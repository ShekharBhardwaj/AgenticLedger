# Riverside Bank dispute policy (invented for this demo)

This policy is fictional and exists only for this exercise.

DP-1 Scope. Applies to debit card disputes raised by personal customers.

DP-2 Classification. Classify every dispute under the network reason code
that fits the customer's statement and the evidence. Recurring charges the
customer set up are cancellations, not disputes (DP-6).

DP-3 Provisional credit timing. Provisional credit for an unauthorized
transaction must be posted within 10 business days of the day the dispute
was received. For accounts opened fewer than 30 days before the dispute,
within 2 business days. Business days exclude Saturdays, Sundays and
national holidays; there are none in March 2026.

DP-4 Provisional credit amount.
DP-4.1 Credit the full disputed amount for an unauthorized transaction when
the card was in the customer's possession.
DP-4.2 Unauthorized card-not-present transaction with the card in the
customer's possession: credit the full amount under network reason code
10.4. Do not include other transactions from the same merchant that the
customer acknowledges as their own.
DP-4.3 Where the customer authorized part of a transaction, credit only the
unauthorized part.

DP-5 Escalation. Escalate to the fraud team, and withhold provisional
credit until their review, when the customer has had a dispute against the
same merchant resolved against them within the previous 12 months, or when
three or more disputes were raised in the previous 12 months.

DP-6 Recurring charges. A recurring charge the customer set up is handled
as a cancellation request, not a dispute, even if the customer wants it
refunded.

DP-7 Memo. Every decision memo states: amount, posting deadline, clause and
reason code, escalation decision with reason, and the documents relied on.
